Dino Merge Support

Need help with Dino Merge on iPhone or iPad? Start with the steps below. The game does not require an account, and game progress is saved on your device.

Restore an ad-free subscription

Open the game's Settings, choose Restore purchases, and use the Apple Account that bought the subscription. If your subscription is active but ads still appear, close and reopen the game, then try Restore purchases again.

Manage or cancel a subscription

Open Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions on your device, then select Dino Merge. Subscriptions are billed and managed by Apple. For an App Store billing or refund request, visit Apple's Report a Problem.

Game progress

Progress is stored on this device. Removing the app or resetting progress inside the game can remove local progress. Dino Merge does not have an account-based cloud save or a way to restore deleted local progress.

Privacy and advertising

Read the Dino Merge Privacy Policy. If advertising privacy options are required in your region, you can reopen them from the game's Settings.

Contact support

To report a bug or ask for help, open a support request on GitHub. GitHub requires a free account to submit a request. Include your device model, iOS version, and a brief description of what happened. Support requests are public, so do not include passwords, purchase receipts, or other personal information.
