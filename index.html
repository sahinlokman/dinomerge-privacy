Dino Merge Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Dino Merge stores your game progress and audio preferences on your device. This includes coins, dinosaurs, discoveries, and settings. The game does not require an account. Dino Merge does not upload this local game data.

Advertising

The free version can display banner ads through Google AdMob. Google's advertising services may process information such as IP address, device identifiers, ad impressions, interactions, crash information, and performance data to deliver, measure, and improve ads. Depending on your region, Dino Merge asks for advertising consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before requesting ads. Where required, you can revisit your advertising choices using Ad privacy options in the game's Settings. Ad-free subscribers do not see banner ads.

See Google's Privacy Policy and its Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.

Subscriptions

Ad-free subscriptions are purchased through Apple's App Store and managed with RevenueCat. Apple handles payment information. RevenueCat creates an anonymous app user identifier and receives purchase and subscription status information needed to determine whether ad-free access is active and to restore it. Dino Merge does not receive your full payment card details. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple account settings.

See Apple's Privacy Policy and RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

Retention and choices

Game progress remains on your device until you reset it in the app or remove the app. Subscription records are retained by Apple and RevenueCat according to their respective policies. Ad-related data and consent records are handled by Google according to its policies and your choices.

Contact

For questions about this policy, visit Dino Merge Support. Support requests are public, so please do not include personal or sensitive information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version and its effective date will appear at this URL.
